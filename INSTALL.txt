EQUAL-LOUDNESS PROCESSOR - MAC INSTALL

App
  Drag "Equal-Loudness Processor.app" into Applications and open it.
  If macOS says it can't verify the developer: open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
  scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Equal-Loudness Processor.
  Allow microphone access when asked (this is how the app reads your interface inputs).

Audio setup (Options > Audio/MIDI Settings)
  Choose your interface and enable 5 inputs and 3 outputs.
  Inputs 1-2  program L/R from the console
  Inputs 3-5  measurement mics 1-3
  Outputs 1-2 mains L/R
  Output 3    sub
  Use 48 kHz and the smallest buffer that runs without dropouts (128 or 256).

Plug-ins (optional)
  AU:   copy the .component to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  VST3: copy the .vst3 to       ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
